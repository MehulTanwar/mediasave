Privacy notice

No accounts, no tracking

MediaSave has no sign-up, no ads and no analytics scripts. We never ask for your passwords or cookies from any platform.

Files are temporary

Your file is stored on the server only so your browser can fetch it. It is deleted automatically after 5 minutes, or sooner if the server restarts.

Minimal logs

Server logs record the site name (for example, “youtube.com”) and whether a request succeeded. They do not record full links or file contents. Your IP address is held in memory for about a minute to limit abuse.

Hosting providers

The companies that host this site and its server may keep their own technical logs. Check their policies if that matters to you.

Frequently asked questions

What can I download here?

Public videos and audio that you own, that are in the public domain, that carry a licence allowing reuse, or that the owner has given you permission to save.

Can I download private, paid or members-only media?

No. MediaSave never logs in on your behalf, so private, paywalled, subscriber-only and age-gated media will fail with a clear message.

Does it work with DRM-protected content?

No. Films and shows from streaming services use DRM. MediaSave does not remove or work around it.

Why does my link say it's unavailable in my region?

MediaSave does not hide its location or use proxies. If a platform restricts media by country, the link will not work here.

What are the size and length limits?

Files up to 200 MB and 60 minutes. Playlists, channels and live streams are not supported. Paste a link to one item at a time.

Which formats do I get?

Video is saved as MP4 where available. Audio is saved in the platform's original audio format, usually M4A or WebM. We don't convert files.

The first download took a long time. Why?

MediaSave runs on free hosting that sleeps when idle. The first request after a quiet period can take up to a minute while the server wakes up.

Is downloading legal?

That depends on who owns the media, your country's laws and the platform's terms. MediaSave is a tool for content you have rights to. You are responsible for how you use it.
