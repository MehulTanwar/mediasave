About MediaSave

MediaSave is a small, free tool for saving a copy of video or audio you have the right to keep: your own uploads, public-domain media, openly licensed work, or files someone has given you permission to download.

What it will not do

MediaSave is built around respect for the access rules that platforms and creators set. It will not:

log in to any account or accept cookies and passwords; download private, paid, members-only or age-gated media; remove or work around DRM; hide its location to get past regional blocks; or fetch playlists, channels and live streams in bulk.

When a link needs any of those things, MediaSave stops and tells you why.

How it works

The website you are looking at is a static front end. When you press Download, it asks a separate server to read the public information for your link, then to fetch a single file into temporary storage. You receive a one-time link to that file, and the server deletes it after five minutes.

Your responsibility

You must have the right to download what you request. Copyright law and platform terms differ by country and by site, and MediaSave cannot check ownership for you. The confirmation box exists as a reminder, not as a guarantee. If you are unsure, ask the owner or choose media that is clearly licensed for reuse.

Copyright or abuse concerns

If you believe MediaSave is being used to infringe your rights, contact the person who runs this deployment. Replace this paragraph with your own contact address before going live.
